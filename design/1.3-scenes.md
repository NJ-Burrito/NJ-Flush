# NJ-Flush 1.3 scenes: 12 variants, beats and sound cues (2026-11-27, round 2)

Owner: "CPU and GPU get 3-4 animations like RAM, with sounds; rework Flush all, its design and animation are bad."
This file is the contract between `Scenes.cs` (motion) and `make_sfx_scenes.py` (sound). **All times are real milliseconds
from the moment the scene is first on screen** (no SPEED factor, unlike the RAM toilet). The scene's wav starts at 0 ms with
the animation. After the last beat the real work runs while the scene idles silently; then the result shows.

Every variant has one `scene-<kind>-<n>.wav` (mono 44.1 kHz, peak at or under 10% full scale, same rule as `make_sfx.py`),
as long as the variant plus a 300 ms tail. Motion follows NJ-Kit `nj-ui-motion-laws` (anticipation before every big move,
one primary motion at a time, follow-through, arcs, a small secondary flourish at the end).

## Which variant plays

| Kind | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CPU (CPU % at flush, or apps busy a full minute, whichever is higher) | Breeze: under 40%, none busy | Vent: 40-69%, or 1 busy | Overheat: 70-89%, or 2 busy | Meltdown: 90%+, or 3+ busy |
| GPU (VRAM in use) | Smudge: under 60% | Steamy: 60-84% | Fogged: 85-94% | Frozen: 95%+ |
| All (the highest of the RAM, CPU and GPU tiers) | Rinse | Shower | Scrub | Flood |
